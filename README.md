# Awesome Nichijou

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)
[![PRs Welcome](https://img.shields.io/badge/PRs-welcome-brightgreen.svg?style=flat-square)](#contributing)

A curated list of useful resources for **Nichijou** (日常, *My Ordinary Life*), the surreal comedy manga by **Keiichi Arawi** and its Kyoto Animation anime adaptation.

> "The ordinary days we take for granted may be a series of miracles."

## Official Sources

- [Kyoto Animation Works Page](https://www.kyotoanimation.co.jp/works/nichijou/) - Official KyoAni entry with story, staff, and cast information.
- [Official Anime Site: Shinonome Lab](https://shinonome-lab.com/) - The original anime website, themed after the Shinonome Lab.
- [KyoAni Shop!](https://kyoanishop.com/) - Kyoto Animation's official store. Search for `日常` to find currently available goods.

## Watch and Read

### Anime

- [Crunchyroll](https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/GYGG1X0QY/nichijou-my-ordinary-life) - Official English-subtitled streaming, availability varies by region.
- [Amazon Prime Video Japan](https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/B09LMFHVFB) - Japanese-region streaming and purchase page.
- [d Anime Store](https://animestore.docomo.ne.jp/) - Japanese anime streaming service. Search for `日常` because its title URLs can change.

### Manga

- [BookWalker Japan](https://bookwalker.jp/series/195/) - Official Japanese digital manga volumes.
- [Kodansha US](https://kodansha.us/series/nichijou/) - Official English manga release.
- [Kadokawa Taiwan](https://www.kadokawa.com.tw/search?keyword=%E6%97%A5%E5%B8%B8) - Traditional Chinese releases from Taiwan Kadokawa.

## Reference Databases

- [Wikipedia: EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nichijou), [JA](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%97%A5%E5%B8%B8_(%E6%BC%AB%E7%94%BB)), [ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E6%97%A5%E5%B8%B8) - General encyclopedia summaries.
- [Moegirlpedia](https://zh.moegirl.org.cn/%E6%97%A5%E5%B8%B8(%E6%BC%AB%E7%94%BB)) - Chinese wiki with character, episode, and meme notes.

- [Kaidomi: Nichijou](https://kaidomi.com/en/title/nichijou-my-ordinary-life--anime-10165) - Free title hub connecting the anime and manga, with staff, character, and voice-actor references.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Please keep the list easy to verify and maintain:

- Prefer official pages, stable database entries, and public fan projects.
- Link to a Nichijou-specific page when one exists; avoid generic homepages.
- Do not add piracy links, private mirrors, or resources that require unclear access.
- Avoid volatile claims such as current ratings, view counts, or store inventory.
- Add a short description that explains why the resource is useful.

Before submitting a pull request, check the [awesome list guidelines](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome/blob/main/contributing.md).
