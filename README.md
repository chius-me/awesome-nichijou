## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Manga** | *Nichijou* (日常) by Keiichi Arawi — serialized in *Monthly Shōnen Ace* (2006–2015; resumed 2021–2025, currently on hiatus), 12 volumes published |
| **OVA** | *Nichijou Episode 0* (日常の0話) — released March 12, 2011 with Manga Vol. 6 Limited Edition |
| **TV Anime** | 26 episodes (April 3 – September 25, 2011) + 12-episode NHK E-tele re-edit (2012) |
| **Animation Studio** | Kyoto Animation — Director: Tatsuya Ishihara · Assistant Director: Taichi Ishidate · Series Composition: Jukki Hanada · Character Design: Futoshi Nishiya · Music: Yuji Nomi |
| **Video Game** | *Nichijou: Uchuujin* (日常〈宇宙人〉, PSP, July 28, 2011, Kadokawa Shoten) |

## Music

> **Discography note:** Yuji Nomi's orchestral score (recorded in Budapest, Hungary) was not released as a single standalone OST album; instead, it was bundled across 13 limited-edition Blu-ray/DVD bonus CDs (*Nichijou no BGM & Radio Bangumi* Vols. 1–13).

## Animation Craft, Interviews and Essays

### Sakuga & Production

- [Sakugabooru — `nichijou` Tag](https://www.sakugabooru.com/post?tags=nichijou) - Curated library of key animation cuts from the TV series and OVA, indexed by animator and technique.
- [Sakuga Blog — *From Nichijou to CITY: Kyoto Animation's Gentle Touch Adapting Keiichi Arawi's Madness*](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2024/09/21/from-nichijou-to-city-kyoto-animations-gentle-touch-adapting-keiichi-arawis-madness/) - Deep production retrospective examining how Tatsuya Ishihara's team translated Arawi's surreal gags into KyoAni's analog craft.
- [Wave Motion Cannon — *Nichijou: Raising the Bar*](https://wavemotioncannon.com/2016/11/01/nichijou-raising-the-bar/) - Detailed English essay analyzing *Nichijou*'s visual comedy, exaggeration, and animation ambition.

### Criticism & Special Publications

- [*Eureka* (ユリイカ) Sept 2025 — Keiichi Arawi Special Issue (Seidosha)](https://www.seidosha.co.jp/book/4054.html) - Dedicated literary and manga criticism volume (*ISBN 978-4-7917-0466-8*) covering *Nichijou*, *CITY*, and *Amemiya-san*, including a long interview with Arawi and critical essays.
- [Animation World Network — *Kyoto Animation Does Sketch Comedy: 'Nichijou' and 'City'*](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/kyoto-animation-does-sketch-comedy-nichijou-and-city) - Comparative essay tracing Kyoto Animation's comedic evolution across both Arawi adaptations.
- [Pop History — *Nichijou Beyond Comedy*](https://pophistory.club/analysis/nichijou-beyond-comedy/) - Long-form reflection on *Nichijou*'s nostalgia, impermanence, and quiet emotional core beneath its absurdism.
- [アニメ『日常』再評価の歴史 (Hatena Blog)](https://aliis.hatenablog.com/entry/2022/05/14/015610) - Japanese critical essay chronicling how *Nichijou* shifted from modest initial disc sales to enduring cult-classic status.

## Games, Spin-offs and Open Source

### *Nichijou: Uchuujin* (PSP, 2011)

- [UchuujinTranslate — English Translation Project (GitHub)](https://github.com/UchuujinTranslate/uchuujin) - Open-source community localization project translating the Japan-only PSP adventure game *Nichijou: Uchuujin* (300+ original scenarios supervised by Keiichi Arawi).
- [UchuujinTranslate Organization (GitHub)](https://github.com/UchuujinTranslate) - Companion reverse-engineering and localization toolchain, including `UchuujinPatchScripts`, `NichiOCR`, and `NichiCGStitcher`.

### Related Works by Keiichi Arawi

- [*Helvetica Standard* (BookWalker Japan)](https://bookwalker.jp/series/377/list/) - Full-color 4-koma anthology serialized in *Newtype* that directly inspired the *Helvetica Standard* segments in the *Nichijou* anime.
- [*Nichijou no Natsuyasumi* (BookWalker Japan)](https://bookwalker.jp/dee9c8eb19-a764-45da-bfe0-16d75fdd2fac/) - Official Kadokawa Tsubasa Bunko light novel spin-off illustrated by Keiichi Arawi.
- [*CITY* / *CITY THE ANIMATION*](https://github.com/chius-me/awesome-citytheanimation) - Spiritual successor sharing subtle world links with *Nichijou*, also animated by Kyoto Animation (2025).
- [*Amemiya-san* (BookWalker Japan)](https://bookwalker.jp/series/390388/list/) - Keiichi Arawi's *Gessan* manga evolved from his self-animated YouTube shorts.

## Real-World Locations (Pilgrimage)

> Tokisadame City and Tokisadame High School are modeled on Keiichi Arawi's hometown of **Isesaki, Gunma Prefecture** (notably Isesaki Station, Gunma Prefectural Isesaki Commercial High School, and Kezoji Park), with selected background references around **Uji, Kyoto** near Kyoto Animation.

- [AniTabi — Nichijou Pilgrimage Map](https://anitabi.jp/works/81?lang=en) - Interactive English/Japanese map of key real-life spots in Isesaki and Maebashi, Gunma.
- [Isesaki City Official — 20th Anniversary × *Nichijou* Collaboration Press Briefing](https://www.city.isesaki.lg.jp/soshiki/kikakubu/koho/kouhou/kaiken/R6/20730.html) - Official municipal record of Isesaki City's first official collaboration with *Nichijou* (2024).
- [TV Anime *Nichijou* Scene-by-Scene Pilgrimage List (Seesaa Wiki)](https://seesaawiki.jp/w/lsh_er/d/TV%A5%A2%A5%CB%A5%E1%A1%D6%C6%FC%BE%EF%A1%D7%C9%F1%C2%E6%C3%B5%CB%AC%A1%CA%C0%BB%C3%CF%BD%E4%CE%E9%A1%CBList) - Exhaustive Japanese cut-by-cut location database covering both Isesaki and Uji.
- [Isesaki Pilgrimage Photo Comparison (そうだ、聖地に行こう。)](http://blog.livedoor.jp/seichijunrei/archives/51984526.html) - Classic 2013 side-by-side anime vs. real-world street comparison archive.
- [Anime *Nichijou* Pilgrimage in Isesaki — Summer 2025 Report (note)](https://note.com/kumabansei/n/na975cafd2ad7) - Up-to-date field report documenting which locations remain intact over a decade after broadcast.
