## Easter Eggs & References

Nichijou is packed with hidden details. Some community discoveries:

- Every episode's title card features a unique illustration referencing a manga chapter not adapted into the anime.
- Episode 5: Mio's "boat explosion" scene is a reference to Kurt Vonnegut's *The Sirens of Titan* (visible as a book on the floor).
- The K-On! cameo: Yui's guitar case appears in a background shot.
- The "Shrine Incident" (Ep. ?) contains frame-by-frame sakuga that blew the show's animation budget.

> **Tip:** Search `nichijou easter egg` on YouTube, Reddit, or Bilibili for community-compiled lists.
